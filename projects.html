Ramie Moth Biocontrol

2025 to present · Live insect rearing, Field work, camera trap review, Python, R

A study of the invasive ramie moth on Oʻahu. Lab colonies were reared and specimens at various stages of life were released in controlled areas to identify a potential biocontrol agent as part of the state of Hawaiʻi's project to protect the native māmaki tree.

Episimus Life Tables

2025 to present · Live insect rearing, Field Work, Python, R

A study of the life stages of the Episimus moth and how they interact with the invasive Christmas berry tree to evaluate their effectiveness as a biocontrol agent since their establishment on the islands in the 1950s.
