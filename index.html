Research Interests

I study how natural enemies, including predators and biocontrol agents, shape the distribution and behavior of their prey, with a focus on invasive species in Hawaiʻi. I pair field data with spatial and quantitative methods to inform conservation where invasive species are changing communities.

My background combines wildlife biology and computer science. I have reared live insect colonies, reviewed camera trap footage, trained machine learning models on field recordings, and built the sensors and web tools that support this work.
